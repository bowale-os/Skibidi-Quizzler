# Skibidi Quizzles 🎯

**Skibidi Quizzles** is an interactive Python-based quiz application designed to test and expand your knowledge in a fun and engaging way. Whether you're preparing for an exam, learning something new, or just passing time, Skibidi Quizzles provides a challenging and enjoyable quiz experience.

## 🚀 Features
- **Interactive Quiz**: Answer multiple-choice or true/false questions and instantly see if your answer is correct or incorrect.
- **Smart Quiz Logic**: The app tracks your correct and incorrect answers, making it easier for you to review your performance.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: A simple and intuitive graphical user interface that makes it easy to navigate through questions and see your results.
- **Multiple Categories**: The quiz can be customized with various question categories (optional depending on implementation).
- **Score Summary**: After completing the quiz, a summary of your score is displayed, helping you gauge your knowledge level.
